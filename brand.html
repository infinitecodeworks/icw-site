Mark and wordmark

Infinite Codeworks

The mark is two interlocked rings reading as an infinity: drawn geometry, never a typed ∞ character. Ring centres sit 2.11 path radii apart (with the stroke centred on the path) so the rings kiss with a small clean junction. Stroke weight matches Archivo Bold. Spruce mark with ink wordmark on light grounds; paper mark on deep spruce grounds.

Type

Display and headings: Archivo (600 or 700, slight negative tracking)
Turn complex technology into plain decisions.
Body: Source Serif 4 (400, 600 for emphasis)
Body copy is set in Source Serif 4. Substitutes where the brand fonts are unavailable are listed below.
Word and OutlookArial Bold for headings, Georgia for body.
Xero (invoices, quotes)Work Sans throughout, the closest available match to Archivo. PT Serif only where a document wants the serif feel. Never Inter.

Documents (proposals, SoWs, reports)

Title and headingsArchivo Bold (or Arial Bold), ink. Heading 2 in spruce is permitted for section leads.
BodySource Serif 4 or Georgia, 10.5 to 11pt, ink.
AccentSpruce #1B5244 only: rules, table header fills (with paper text), links. Never more than one accent colour per document.
TablesHairline borders in #D7DAD1, header row deep spruce with paper text.
CalloutsTint #EBEEE7 background with a 3pt spruce left border.
Logologo.png top left of page one, roughly 45mm wide. Never stretched, recoloured or set on a busy image.

Word document build spec (proposals, SoWs, reports)

Authoritative values for generated .docx documents. Measurements in twips (1440 per inch) unless stated. This spec supersedes the general logo sizing note above for Word documents.

Page (A4)Margins 1440 twips left and right, 1080 twips top and bottom.
FontsHeadings Arial Bold, body Georgia. All text defaults to Ink #191D1A.
Type scaleDocument title 15pt Spruce Arial Bold. H1 12pt Spruce Arial Bold. H2 11pt Spruce Arial Bold. Body 10.5pt Georgia, justified. Table cells 9.5pt Georgia. Meta (date, ref, version) 10pt. Small and footer 8.5pt.
Paragraph spacingBody: line spacing 320 twips (about 1.33x), 160 after. H1: 320 before, 140 after. H2: 300 before, 120 after.
Section heading tint barsSingle-cell borderless table, full content width, Tint #EBEEE7 background, padding 200 top and bottom, 140 left and right.
Callout boxesSingle-cell table, full width, Tint background, left border only 6pt Spruce, padding 160 top and bottom, 200 left, 160 right. Body text italic. Spacer 200 above, 320 below.
Data tablesHeader row Deep Spruce background with Paper bold text. Cell borders 1pt Rule #D7DAD1. Padding 40 top and bottom, 80 left and right. Cells 9.5pt Georgia.
Signature blockTwo equal columns, Tint background, outer border 4pt Rule, vertical divider 2pt Rule, padding 180 top and bottom, 160 left and right. Labels Spruce bold at body size. 18pt spacer between label and signature line, 6pt between name and date. Include when the document needs signing.
Header areaLogo at 255x24px, left aligned, 340 after. Title 15pt Arial Bold Spruce, 80 after. Meta lines 10pt Stone, 120 after. Horizontal rule 12pt in Rule colour with 4 space, then 360 after.
