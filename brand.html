Type

Display and headings: Archivo (600 or 700, slight negative tracking)
Turn complex technology into plain decisions.
Body: Source Serif 4 (400, 600 for emphasis)
Body copy is set in Source Serif 4. In Word or Outlook where it is not installed, use Georgia. For headings where Archivo is unavailable, use Arial Bold. In Xero (invoices and quotes) use Work Sans throughout, the closest available match to Archivo; PT Serif only where a document wants the serif feel.

Documents (proposals, SoWs, reports)

Title and headingsArchivo Bold (or Arial Bold), ink. Heading 2 in spruce is permitted for section leads.
BodySource Serif 4 or Georgia, 10.5 to 11pt, ink.
AccentSpruce #1B5244 only: rules, table header fills (with paper text), links. Never more than one accent colour per document.
TablesHairline borders in #D7DAD1, header row deep spruce with paper text.
CalloutsTint #EBEEE7 background with a 3pt spruce left border.

Voice

Plain EnglishIf it cannot be said plainly, it is not ready. No jargon a board would not use.
Evidence over adjectivesClaims carry numbers, names or working prototypes. Prefer "measured" to "world-class".
PunctuationNo em-dashes. Commas, colons or full stops. No Oxford comma. Dates as DD Mon.
